If you're a contractor in Shelby or greater Cleveland County, your Google Business Profile often decides whether you get the call or the competitor does. Setup is free. Mis-setup is expensive.

## Answer-first summary

For Shelby contractors: claim the profile, pick one accurate primary category, list only services you actually offer, use real job photos, and ask for reviews after every completed job. Do not fake locations or review packs.

## Setup steps that matter

1. **Claim and verify** with the postcard, phone, or video options Google offers.
2. **Name:** use your real business name — no keyword-stuffed "Shelby Roofing Best Cheap" names (policy risk).
3. **Categories:** one primary; secondary only for real services.
4. **Service area:** towns you actually serve (Shelby, Kings Mountain, Boiling Springs, Gastonia, etc.).
5. **Services:** mirror how customers search (metal roof repair, storm damage, gutters).
6. **Photos:** trucks, job sites before/after, team (with permission).
7. **Website link:** point to a page that matches the service, not only the homepage.

## Ongoing habits

- Answer Q&A on the profile.
- Reply to reviews within a few days.
- Update hours for holidays.
- Track calls and direction requests monthly so you know which months the profile earned its keep.
