Reviews influence both the map pack and whether a human trusts you enough to call. Here's how to get them the sustainable way.

## Answer-first summary

Ask right after a job well done, send a direct link to your Google review form, reply to every review, and never buy, gate, or incentivize reviews. Steady beats sudden.

## The timing that works

- Ask when the customer is happiest: project completion, delivery, or a compliment.
- Follow up once — a single polite reminder.
- Make it easy: direct review link, QR code on a card or invoice.

## A simple ask script

> "Glad we got this sorted for you. If you have 30 seconds, an honest Google review helps other folks in Shelby find us. Here's the direct link — no forms, no login tricks. Either way, thanks for choosing us."

Keep it optional and neutral. Never condition the review on a reward.

## Replying

- Thank specifics ("Thanks for noticing the timeline").
- Address complaints calmly and take them offline.
- Your replies are public proof you're responsive.

## What to avoid

- Review gates ("rate us 5 stars or tell us privately first")
- Paid review services
- Review swaps with other businesses
