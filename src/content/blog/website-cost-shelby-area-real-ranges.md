Shopping for a website in the Shelby area? The honest answer is not a single number — but you can get clear ranges and know what moves the price.

## Answer-first summary

In the Shelby area, a professional small-business website often lands between about $500 and $2,000 to launch when paired with a care plan, or higher for large custom builds. Monthly hosting, security, and SEO typically add $150–$600/month. Page count, e-commerce, and content complexity drive most of the difference.

## What makes a project cost more

- More pages or unique layouts
- E-commerce or booking systems
- Custom copywriting or photography
- Complex migrations from old sites

## What usually does not help the price

- Endless revision cycles without decisions
- "Make it like Brand X" without a clear goal
- Paying for features you will not use in year one
