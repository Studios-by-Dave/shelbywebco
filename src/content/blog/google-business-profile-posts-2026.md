GBP posts are easy to overestimate. They are not magic ranking fuel — they are cheap, real estate on the surface where many customers decide.

## Answer-first summary

Post weekly if you have genuine updates (offers, completed projects, events). Do not expect posts to rank you by themselves — reviews, categories, and your website do the heavy lifting. Posts keep the profile alive and give searchers a reason to act.

## What to post

- Seasonal offers (link to a real landing page)
- Before/after job photos with a caption
- Short tips relevant to your trade
- Holiday hours and closures

## What to skip

- Repetitive keyword-stuffed posts
- Fake urgency
- Posts that promise things your site can't back up

I would rather see no posts than filler. An empty quarter is better than a quarter of noise.

## How this fits the system

Profile → posts/photos → reviews → service pages on your site → schema. Each piece corroborates the others for both Google and AI assistants.
