If you run a local business in Gastonia, do three things first: claim and complete your Google Business Profile, give each service you offer its own page on your website, and build a steady habit of collecting real customer reviews. Do those before you spend money on anything else.

## The 90-day checklist

You don't need a huge budget. You need to do a few things well and in the right order.

### Days 1 to 30: get found

1. **Claim and complete your Google Business Profile.** Add your exact business name, address, phone number, hours, service areas, categories and real photos of your work. This is the single biggest local lever for most small businesses.
2. **Make your business details match everywhere.** Your name, address and phone number should be written the same way on your website, Google, Facebook and any directory you're listed in. Mismatches confuse search engines and customers.
3. **Check your website works on a phone.** If it's slow or hard to read on a phone, fix that before anything else.

### Days 31 to 60: get clear

1. **Give each main service its own page.** One page that lists everything you do is hard for Google to understand. A page for "HVAC repair in Gastonia" and another for "HVAC installation in Gastonia" tells Google and customers exactly what you offer.
2. **Write for the person searching.** Open each page by answering the question they're asking, mention Gastonia and the nearby areas you serve, and make your phone number and contact form easy to find.
3. **Add a few real questions and answers to each service page**, such as what it costs, how long it takes and what areas you cover.

### Days 61 to 90: get trusted

1. **Ask for reviews every time.** Ask happy customers right after you finish the job, and make it easy with a direct link to your Google review page. Reply to every review, good or bad. Never buy reviews or offer rewards for them.
2. **Post regularly to your Google Business Profile.** A short update with a real photo of recent work shows you're active.
3. **Look at your results.** Open Google Search Console and see which searches show your site. Note which pages get impressions but few clicks, and improve their titles and descriptions first.

## Mistakes I see local businesses make

- A website that only says "we serve the area." Name the towns you actually work in.
- One page for every service. Break it up.
- Ignoring reviews. Unanswered reviews cost you trust.
- Trying to rank in too many towns at once. Win Gastonia first, then expand to nearby towns.
- Treating SEO as a one-time project. Rankings come from steady work over months, not a single weekend of changes.

## What to expect

Local SEO takes time. Most changes take weeks to be noticed and a few months to show up in your calls and form fills. Anyone who promises a first-page ranking in a few days isn't being straight with you. Track your progress monthly and judge it by calls, form fills and visits, not only rankings.
