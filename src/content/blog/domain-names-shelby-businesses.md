Buying a domain is the easy part. Choosing one you won't regret in five years takes a bit more thought.

## Answer-first summary

Pick a short, spellable .com you can say over the phone without confusion. Don't stuff your town into it. Keep the brand, put locality in content and your Google profile.

## What matters

- **Sayability:** can customers repeat it after hearing it once?
- **Length and spelling:** avoid hyphens and odd spellings.
- **Email:** a matching domain for professional email is worth it.
- **Renewal control:** register it in your (or your company's) account, not a contractor's personal account.

## What doesn't matter much

- Town names in the domain
- Exact-match keywords
- Buying multiple "defensive" domains you'll never use
