## Why I'm doing this in Shelby

This company grew up in Shelby, and a lot of the people who helped it grow own the small businesses that make this area what it is. Over the past couple of years I've seen quite a lot of the same thing: a good local business with an outdated website, or no website at all, quietly losing customers to whoever shows up first on Google.

So this fall, I'm doing something about it. Five businesses across Cleveland and Gaston County will get a brand-new, custom website, designed, built and launched by Shelby Web Company, with the build fee covered. Builds like this are work I'd normally charge for, and I'm doing it as a thank-you to the local businesses that have supported this company since the beginning. It helps you, and it helps me build this business's foundation the right way. Either way, I'd rather show you what a good website does than tell you.

## The deal, in plain numbers

No surprises, so here is exactly how it works:

- I cover the **$497** build and launch fee. That's the design, build and launch of a custom 4 to 5 page website. It's work I've previously priced at about $1,200 on average.
- You continue on our Core plan at **$297/month for 12 months** after launch — cancel at any time. That's a total commitment of $3,564 over the year, and I want you to know that before you apply. This isn't a build-and-goodbye deal: your site keeps improving with ongoing SEO, fresh content and regular updates.
- Core isn't a basic care plan. Most website care plans run around $150 a month for hosting and maintenance. Core adds ongoing SEO and AI improvements plus continued content creation.

## What's included in Core

- A 4 to 5 page, mobile-first website
- Hosting, SSL, backups and security
- Contact forms and lead alerts
- Google Analytics and Search Console
- Foundational on-page and local SEO
- One small monthly edit request
- A monthly performance snapshot and a quarterly growth review

## Who should apply

The program is for local businesses in the areas we serve, solo operators and small teams alike — Shelby, Gastonia, Kings Mountain, Boiling Springs, Forest City and the surrounding area. You qualify if your website is old, slow, hard to find on Google, or doesn't exist yet. You need to be open for business and ready to launch during the program window.

Home service companies, contractors, restaurants, boutiques, health and wellness businesses, professional services and nonprofits are all welcome.

## What I ask in return

This costs me real money, so I ask four things of winners:

1. **An honest testimonial.** Tell the truth, good and nitpicky. It becomes your case study.
2. **A Google review** once your site is live and you're happy with it.
3. **Permission to feature your business for 6 months.** You approve everything before it goes out.
4. **One warm introduction** to another owner who could use a better website.

## What happens after you win

I'll start with a short pre-discovery call, then we design, build and launch your site in weeks, not months. After that, every Golden Ticket site is tracked from day one as a living case study:

- **Day 30:** site live, Analytics running, lead forms delivering and local SEO in place.
- **Day 60:** your first calls and form fills arrive, and we tune pages around what's working.
- **Day 90:** a clean results snapshot covering traffic, leads and rankings.

I'll share what we learn, with your approval, right here on the blog, so other local owners can see what a website can really do.
