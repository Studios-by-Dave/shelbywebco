> **Offer paused:** The Golden Ticket giveaway is not currently accepting applications. This article is preserved as a record of the original proposal; see [current website pricing](/pricing/) for active offers.

The original Golden Ticket proposal was for three local businesses to receive a custom website with the $597 setup fee covered. The proposed continuation was the $297/month Core plan, month-to-month with 30 days' written notice to cancel.

## Why I'm doing this in Shelby

This company grew up in Shelby, and a lot of the people who helped it grow own the small businesses that make this area what it is. Over the past couple of years I've seen quite a lot of the same thing: a good local business with an outdated website, or no website at all, quietly losing customers to whoever shows up first on Google.

The proposal was to support three businesses across Cleveland and Gaston County with a brand-new, custom website designed, built and launched by Shelby Web Company. It was intended as a thank-you to the local businesses that have supported the company since the beginning.

## The original proposal, in plain numbers

The proposed terms were:

- The **$597** setup fee would be covered. It included design, build and launch of a custom 4 to 5 page website.
- Selected businesses would continue on the Core plan at **$297/month** after launch — month-to-month, with 30 days’ written notice to cancel.
- Core was distinct from the $149/month Website Care Plan, which covers hosting, backups, edits, and updates only.

## What the proposed Core plan included

- A 4 to 5 page, mobile-first website
- Hosting, SSL, backups and security
- Contact forms and lead alerts
- Google Analytics and Search Console
- Foundational on-page and local SEO
- One small monthly edit request
- A monthly performance snapshot and a quarterly growth review

## Who the original offer was for

The original program was intended for local businesses in the areas we serve, solo operators and small teams alike — Shelby, Gastonia, Kings Mountain, Boiling Springs, Forest City and the surrounding area. The offer is now paused.

Home service companies, contractors, restaurants, boutiques, health and wellness businesses, professional services and nonprofits are all welcome.

## How the original proposal planned to choose businesses

The proposed selection criteria were story and need: where a modern website could genuinely change the next 12 months for a business. The offer is paused, and no active selection process is underway.

## What the original proposal asked in return

The proposal asked selected businesses for four things:

1. **An honest testimonial** after the work.
2. **A Google review** once the site was live and the business was happy with it.
3. **Permission to feature the business for 6 months**, with approval before publication.
4. **One warm introduction** to another owner who could use a better website.

## The proposed follow-up plan

The plan was to start with a short pre-discovery call, then design, build and launch each site. Results would be tracked from day one:

- **Day 30:** site live, Analytics running, lead forms delivering and local SEO in place.
- **Day 60:** your first calls and form fills arrive, and we tune pages around what's working.
- **Day 90:** a clean results snapshot covering traffic, leads and rankings.

The proposal was to share results on the blog with each business's approval.

## Current status

The Golden Ticket offer is paused and applications are not being accepted. Visit [current website pricing](/pricing/) or request a [free SEO audit](/promotions/free-seo-audit/).
