We've spent 2026 publishing honest pricing and real local work. The Golden Ticket is the giveback version of that: five local businesses get a brand-new custom website with the build fee on us.

## The offer in one paragraph

We choose **5 local businesses** and build them a custom 4–5 page website. We cover the **$497** design, build, and launch fee — work we've priced around $1,200. Winners continue on the Core plan at **$297/month for 12 months** (hosting, security, edits, ongoing SEO). That's it. No upfront invoice for the build.

## Who should apply

Local businesses in the areas we serve — Shelby, Kings Mountain, Boiling Springs, Polkville, Gastonia, Forest City, and the rest of Cleveland and Gaston counties. Solo operators and small teams. Having no website, an old website, or a website nobody finds all qualify equally well.

## How winners are chosen

Judged, not drawn. A human reads every application and picks the five stories where a modern website genuinely changes the next twelve months. Full details live in the [Official Rules](/promotions/the-golden-ticket/rules/).

## Key dates

- **Applications close:** December 15, 2026, 11:59 PM ET
- **Class of 2026:** 5 recipients, announced after review

## Apply

Applications take about five minutes — story first, no pitch deck. [Apply on the Golden Ticket page](/promotions/the-golden-ticket/).

## What winners get (and what we get)

Recipients continue on the same Core plan every Core client signs. In return we ask for an honest testimonial, a Google review if they're genuinely happy, permission to feature them for six months (they approve everything first), and one warm intro. Nothing shady — it's all in the [Official Rules](/promotions/the-golden-ticket/rules/).
