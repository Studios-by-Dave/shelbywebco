# Lead-Gen Action Tracker — Website Rescue

**Companion to:** `docs/lead-generation-plan.md` (strategy + rationale live there; this is the to-do list).
**Goal:** 5 free audits in month 1 → first 5 rescue clients by ~60–90 days.
**KPI:** audit requests, not downloads.

---

## Phase 1 — Direct, free, local (this month · target: 5 free audits)

### 1. Warm-contact audits (own backyard first)
- [ ] Make a list of 15–20 warm contacts: past clients, Chamber members, businesses you personally use
- [ ] Run the free audit on each (use `docs/audit-template-and-rescue-report.md`)
- [ ] Send the "I searched for you" message (template below) — text or email
- [ ] Track each contact in the Log below

**Message template:**
> Hey [name] — quick thing. I was searching for [their service] in Shelby and noticed your site didn't show up where it should. I do free website check-ups for local businesses — took a look at yours and found a couple of things worth fixing. Want me to send you what I found? No charge, no pitch.

### 2. "I searched for you" screenshot outreach
- [ ] Pick 20 Shelby/Cleveland County service businesses (roofers, HVAC, dentists, auto shops, landscapers)
- [ ] For each: search "city + service", screenshot the results (their absence = the outreach asset)
- [ ] Send the template message + screenshot
- [ ] Expect ~10% cold, 20–30% warm-ish response

### 3. Own visibility checkpoint
- [ ] Own Google Business Profile verified, complete, current
- [ ] Site footer + directory listings consistent (name/address/phone identical everywhere)

## Phase 2 — Publish the funnel assets (weeks 3–6)

- [x] Ship the checklist page → `/website-rescue-checklist/` (live, linked from homepage / audit page / blog layout / footer)
- [ ] Create print/PDF version of the checklist (one page)
- [ ] Share checklist in local Facebook groups — post angle: *"I put together a 5-minute checklist — 15 things to check if your website isn't bringing you business. #3 catches most people."*
- [ ] Write 3 symptom-titled blog posts (each ends: checklist → free audit):
  - [ ] "Why isn't my business showing up on Google? (5 common causes)"
  - [ ] "Is your website AI-built? Here's how to tell — and why it matters"
  - [ ] "Website not getting calls? 7 conversion killers for local businesses"
- [ ] Start weekly GBP posts ("This week's free audit found: sitemap missing…")
- [ ] Ask every audit recipient (even non-buyers) for a Google review of the audit experience

## Phase 3 — Local visibility & referrals (months 2–3)

- [ ] Join Shelby Chamber of Commerce; use intro: *"I help local businesses whose websites aren't showing up on Google or bringing in calls — I do a free check-up to tell you exactly what's wrong."*
- [ ] Partner referrals: bookkeepers, insurance agents, print shops, business photographers
- [ ] Print checklist leave-behind cards (card + your number)
- [ ] Point the $100 referral bonus explicitly at rescue work
- [ ] Start the before/after proof engine: capture search-result, PageSpeed, and form-submission before/afters on every rescue (with permission, anonymized)

## Weekly rhythm (solo-operator realistic)

| Day | Action | This week? |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Send 5 outreach messages (screenshot method) | [ ] |
| Tue–Wed | Deliver 1–2 free audits | [ ] |
| Thu | 1 GBP post + 1 social post | [ ] |
| Fri | Follow up every open outreach + ask for reviews | [ ] |

**Funnel math:** 10 outreach → 1–2 audits → 1 in 3 audits becomes paid work. ~15–20 outreach/week ≈ 2–4 paying rescue clients/month.

## What NOT to do (yet)
- No paid ads until the funnel converts organically
- No "AI watermark removal" branding
- No "your website is broken" cold-email spam — the screenshot-with-a-real-search approach is the honest version that gets replies
