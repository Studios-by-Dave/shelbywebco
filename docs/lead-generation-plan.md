# Deliverable #6 — Lead Generation Plan: First Rescue Clients (Local)

🧠 **Strategy:** your buyer is a local business owner who suspects something's wrong but can't name it. They don't search "website rescue" — they search "why isn't my business showing up on Google." So the plan is: put the *symptom* in front of them, deliver the free audit personally, and let the audit convert. Volume isn't the goal; 10–20 good local businesses are.

---

## Phase 1 — This month: direct, free, local (target: 5 free audits)

### 1. Your own backyard first
- **Run the free audit on businesses you already know** — past clients, Chamber of Commerce members, businesses you personally use. Warm contacts say yes 10x more than cold.
- Message template (text/email):
> Hey [name] — quick thing. I was searching for [their service] in Shelby and noticed your site didn't show up where it should. I do free website check-ups for local businesses — took a look at yours and found a couple of things worth fixing. Want me to send you what I found? No charge, no pitch.

🧠 **Why this works:** you lead with an observed, specific symptom ("didn't show up when I searched") — not "I'm a web agency." You're a helpful neighbor reporting a problem, and the audit is proof.

### 2. The "I searched for you" audit
- Pick 20 Shelby/Cleveland County service businesses (roofers, HVAC, dentists, auto shops, landscapers — your industry pages already target these)
- Search "city + service" for each. Screenshot the results — their absence *is* the outreach asset
- Send the message above with the screenshot. Expect 20–30% response on warm-ish, 10% cold

### 3. Google Business Profile checkpoint
- Make sure your own GBP, site footer, and directory listings are flawless — you're about to be judged on your own search visibility

## Phase 2 — Weeks 3–6: publish the funnel assets

### 4. Ship the checklist (deliverable #3)
- Post as a page + PDF; link from homepage; share in local Facebook groups (Shelby NC community groups are active for this)
- Post angle: *"I put together a 5-minute checklist — 15 things to check if your website isn't bringing you business. #3 catches most people."*

### 5. Symptom-titled content (not service-titled)
Write 3 blog posts framed as the buyer's actual searches:
- "Why isn't my business showing up on Google? (5 common causes)"
- "Is your website AI-built? Here's how to tell — and why it matters"
- "Website not getting calls? 7 conversion killers for local businesses"
Each ends with: free checklist → free audit.

### 6. Google Business Profile posts + reviews
- Weekly GBP post ("This week's free audit found: sitemap missing...") — each is a mini case study without naming names
- Ask every audit recipient (even if they don't buy) for a Google review of the *audit experience*

## Phase 3 — Months 2–3: local visibility & referrals

### 7. Chamber of Commerce / BNI / local networking
- Join the Shelby Chamber if not already. Your one-line intro: *"I help local businesses whose websites aren't showing up on Google or bringing in calls — I do a free check-up to tell you exactly what's wrong."*
- The free audit is the perfect networking offer: everyone can accept a free diagnostic, unlike a sales pitch

### 8. Partner referrals
- Local bookkeepers, insurance agents, print shops, business photographers all talk to your buyers. Offer: reciprocal referral + they look good for bringing a free resource
- One-page leave-behind: "The 5-Minute Website Audit Checklist" card with your number

### 9. Referral bonus (already have it — point it at rescues)
- Your $100 referral bonus: explicitly include "referrals that turn into rescue work" — your early clients know other owners with the same problem

### 10. Before/after proof engine
- Every completed rescue → screenshot evidence: search results before/after, PageSpeed before/after, form-submission counts (with client permission, anonymized)
- These become: homepage proof section (later), GBP posts, social posts, and the case studies that let you raise prices (per deliverable #5)

---

## Weekly rhythm (solo-operator realistic)
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Mon | Send 5 outreach messages (search-screenshot method) |
| Tue–Wed | Deliver 1–2 free audits |
| Thu | 1 GBP post + 1 social post |
| Fri | Follow up every open outreach + ask for reviews |

🧠 **Realistic funnel math:** 10 outreach → 1–2 audits → 1 in 3 audits becomes paid work. So ~15–20 outreach/week ≈ 2–4 paying rescue clients/month within a quarter. That's the first five clients by roughly 60–90 days — enough for the proof you need to raise prices.

## What NOT to do (yet)
- No paid ads until the funnel converts organically (audit → fix conversion is unknown; spend after proof)
- No "AI watermark removal" branding (per strategy)
- Don't spam business owners with "your website is broken" cold emails — the screenshot-with-a-real-search approach is the honest version that actually gets replies
