# Deliverable #3 — Free Lead Magnet Checklist
**Name (recommended):** The 5-Minute Website Audit Checklist
**Alt name:** The Website Rescue Checklist
**Purpose:** Lead magnet that drives downloads → free audit requests. Keep it genuinely useful standalone; it should make the reader slightly worried and then relieved that a free audit exists.

🧠 **Why the shorter name:** "5-Minute" promises a time investment they can actually make, and implies "even you can spot these problems." "Website Rescue Checklist" reinforces the brand. Use both: *The Website Rescue Checklist: 15 Things to Check in 5 Minutes.*

## The Checklist (print-ready content)

**Intro (top of page/PDF):**
> Your website has one job: bring you customers. This quick checklist shows you the 15 things most likely to be broken — the same things we check in our free SEO audit. Grab a coffee and your phone. Five minutes.

### PART 1 — Can Google Find You?
1. **Search your business name + city on Google.** Does your website show up on page one? If not, you have an indexing or SEO problem.
2. **Search "yourcity + your service"** (e.g., "Shelby NC roofing"). Are you anywhere in the results? Are competitors there instead?
3. **Type yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml in your browser.** If nothing loads, Google may not know your pages exist.
4. **Type yourdomain.com/robots.txt.** No result or an error? Crawler instructions may be missing — or accidentally blocking Google.

### PART 2 — Does Your Site Look Trustworthy?
5. **Look at your site on your phone, not your laptop.** Is text readable without zooming? Do buttons work with a thumb?
6. **Is your phone number clickable at the top of your homepage?** Customers call the easiest number to tap.
7. **Count the real photos vs. stock photos.** Sites that feel generic get less trust — and less business.
8. **Read your homepage headline out loud.** Does it say what you do and where? Or does it say something clever but vague?
9. **Check your copyright year in the footer.** A stale year quietly says "nobody's home."

### PART 3 — Does It Bring You Business?
10. **Can a stranger tell what you do in 5 seconds?** Show the homepage to someone outside your industry and ask.
11. **Is there ONE obvious next step** (call, form, book)? Or do three different buttons compete for attention?
12. **Fill out your own contact form.** Does it work? Do you actually get notified?
13. **Do you show reviews, testimonials, or credentials near your call-to-action?** Trust converts.

### PART 4 — The AI Test
14. **Does your site read like everyone else's?** Generic "Welcome to our website, we offer quality solutions" copy is a red flag to customers — and to search engines.
15. **Would you hire you based on this website?** Be honest. That's the real test.

**Outro (bottom):**

## Implementation notes
- **Format:** one-page PDF (downloadable) + HTML version as a blog/listing page for SEO
- **Capture:** email required for download → add them to a simple follow-up sequence ("Did you check your sitemap? Here's what a missing one means... → free audit")
- **Link placement:** homepage (small banner/link), audit landing page, blog posts footer, email signature
- 🧠 Note: if you'd rather keep zero friction, make the checklist a visible page (no email gate) and treat the free audit as the capture. Lower download count, higher audit conversion. Recommend this — your KPI is audit requests, not downloads.
