# Deliverable #5 — Service Menu & Pricing (paid audit + fixes)

🧠 **Pricing logic:** the paid audit must feel small next to the value of fixes, and the fixes must feel small next to a rebuild. Anchoring ladder: Free ($0) → Audit (one-time, low hundreds) → Fixes (project-based) → Rebuild ($497 anchor you already have). Every price should make the next decision easy.

---

## 1. Free SEO Audit — $0
As defined. Delivered as a short written summary (half page) within a few business days. Costs you ~1 hour per audit — that's your marketing budget, spend it on businesses that could plausibly afford fixes.

## 2. Full Website Rescue Audit — $197 (one-time)
**Includes:** everything in the report structure (deliverable #4B): full indexing review, GSC analysis (if access granted), crawlability/sitemap/robots, deeper technical SEO, basic vulnerability review, content/trust/conversion blockers, scorecards, prioritized fix plan (Website Rescue Report, PDF).

**🧠 Key rule: the $197 is credited.** If they proceed with fixes through you within 60 days, the $197 comes off the fix invoice. This makes the audit a low-risk purchase instead of a toll booth — and it filters out freebie-hunters while never penalizing buyers.

**Alternatives considered:**
- $97 — easier yes, but too cheap to signal seriousness; you'd do the same work
- $297–$497 — strong anchor, but it's your *rebuild* price; don't compete with yourself
- Free "now, full audit later" — you already run free; a second free tier has nothing to sell

## 3. Fix Packages (project-based, quoted after the audit)

**A. Visibility Rescue — from $349 one-time**
Indexing repairs, sitemap/robots fixes, GSC setup, technical SEO cleanup, speed fixes on key pages, LocalBusiness schema.
*Roughly: 6–10 hours work. Undercuts a rebuild psychologically: "less than your rebuild price to get found."*

**B. Credibility Rescue (AI Cleanup) — from $299 one-time**
Rewrite AI-generated/robotic copy on up to 5 pages, de-generic styling pass, mobile UX fixes, trust signals (reviews section, credentials, real-photo guidance).

**C. Conversion Rescue — from $299 one-time**
CTA strategy + implementation, lead form rebuild, service-page structure (up to 3 pages), phone/contact optimization, one local landing page.

**D. Full Rescue (A+B+C combined) — from $749** *(saves ~$200 vs. individually)*
The complete rescue: found, credible, converting.

**E. Ongoing Rescue Care — $149/month (existing Website Care Plan, rebranded)**
Hosting, backups, edits, monitoring, monthly performance snapshot.
**F. Ongoing Local SEO — $297/month (existing plan, unchanged)**
Keep exactly as-is; six-to-twelve month expectation language stays.

## 4. Rebuild — $497 one-time + plan (existing pricing, unchanged)
Positioned as "when a fresh start makes sense" — stays the anchor that makes fixes look cheap.

## The price sheet (internal math)
| Tier | Price | Your time | Effective rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free audit | $0 | ~1 hr | Marketing |
| Full Rescue Audit | $197 (credited) | 3–4 hrs | ~$50–65/hr → fine, it's a foot in the door |
| Visibility Rescue | from $349 | 6–10 hrs | ~$35–58/hr → OK solo; raise floor after first 3 clients |
| Full Rescue combo | from $749 | 15–22 hrs | ~$34–50/hr → acceptable early; this is portfolio-building pricing |
| Rebuild | $497 + monthly | existing | unchanged |

🧠 **Honest flag:** these day-rates are low for a solo operator — this is deliberately *market-entry* pricing to win the first 5–10 rescue clients and gather before/after proof. Build the case studies, then raise: the natural move is "from $349 → from $497" and audit $197 → $247 once you have three documented rescues.

🧠 **What NOT to do:** no hourly billing (invites micro-management), no "SEO guaranteed rankings" claims, no bundled single price for build+SEO (your existing two-charge rule stays).
